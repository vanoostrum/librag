Which of the two gives practical advice for decision makers?

  1. Searched your books · 8 passages found
  2. Writing the answer…

Kahneman gives the more concrete advice. He recommends the premortem: before a decision is final, the team imagines it failed a year later and writes down why.1 Taleb advises building exposure to positive surprises and limiting exposure to negative ones, instead of trying to predict them.2

1 · Thinking, Fast and Slow, ch. 24, The Engine of Capitalism “The procedure is simple: when the organization has almost come to an important decision…”
2 · The Black Swan, ch. 13, Appelles the Painter “…maximize the serendipity around you.”
